Add a GIF to your Blenny

Your GIF shows up in Silly Animations. Up to 8 GIFs, 1 MB each.

!
Heads up: this runs over USB serial. That’s the same slow link used to program the badge, so sending a GIF can take anywhere from a few seconds to about a minute, with the odd pause while the badge catches up. Be patient, keep the badge plugged in, and leave this tab open until it says Done.
This page needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop (it talks to the badge over USB). Safari, Firefox and phones can't do that.

1 Connect your badge

Plug the badge in with a USB-C data cable, then pick it from the list (it may show as “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”).

2 Choose a GIF

Drop a .gif here, or click to choose one

3 Send it

Takes a few seconds to a minute depending on size. Keep the badge plugged in and this tab open; short pauses are normal while the badge catches up.
